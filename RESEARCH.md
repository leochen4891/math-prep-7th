# Research: What the 7th-grade Algebra-Readiness placement test covers

This document records the research behind the topic coverage in this project, so future
contributors understand *why* the chapters and difficulty levels are what they are.

## Context

- Livingston Public Schools (NJ) places students into Pre-Algebra 7 or, for a small top
  percentage, **Algebra 1 in 7th grade**. With 500+ students per grade and only the top
  ~5–10% placed into Algebra 1, the bar is competitive.
- **Goal of this site:** *over-prepare.* Cover more than the test is likely to ask, so a
  strong student is comfortably ready.

## What the official Livingston document actually says

The district's "Math Parent Night: Math Sequence Gr. 6-12" presentation describes the
**placement process**, not a test content outline. It names:

- an **"Algebra Readiness Test"** (no topic list given),
- benchmark tools **IXL** and **Orleans-Hanna**,
- non-test criteria: average of Marking Periods 1–3, a teacher **"mathematical practices"**
  rubric, and a review of **NJSLA / CogAT** scores,
- a **placement matrix** combining those.

**There is no public, official list of the test's topics.** Everything below is reconstructed
from authoritative sources.

## How the topic list was reconstructed (two layers)

### Layer A — Prerequisite content (high confidence: NJSLS Grade 6 standards)

The strongest anchor is what NJ 6th-graders are actually taught, since the placement test
draws on those prerequisites. NJSLS Grade 6 domains:

| Domain | Topics |
|---|---|
| Ratios & Proportional Relationships | ratios, unit rates, percent, unit conversion |
| The Number System | dividing fractions, decimals, **GCF/LCM**, integers & rational numbers, **absolute value**, coordinate plane (4 quadrants) |
| Expressions & Equations | exponents, evaluating/writing expressions, equivalent expressions, solving 1-variable **equations & inequalities**, dependent/independent variables |
| Geometry | **area** (triangles/quadrilaterals/polygons), **volume**, **nets & surface area**, polygons on the grid |
| Statistics & Data | statistical questions, distributions, **mean/median/range**, dot plots/histograms/box plots |

### Layer B — Algebra-aptitude items (from Orleans-Hanna & IAAT design)

The readiness tests add an **aptitude / "prognosis"** dimension — they measure *ability to learn
algebra*, not just memorized arithmetic:

- **Orleans-Hanna** (~50 items): includes a *work-sample* component (teaches a short idea, then
  tests applying it) — so reasoning matters as much as computation.
- **IAAT** (60 items, 4 parts): Pre-Algebraic Number Skills · Interpreting Mathematical
  Information (graphs/text) · **Representing Relationships** (find the formula/rule, input-output
  tables, patterns) · Using Symbols (algebraic notation, substitution).

Aptitude skills to train: **find the rule/formula for a pattern**, **input-output / function
tables**, **interpret graphs & tables**, translate words ↔ symbols, multi-step word problems.

## How our content maps to all this

| Chapter | Covers | Source justification |
|---|---|---|
| Fractions, Decimals & Percents | conversions, ops, percent of / change | NJSLS-6 RP & NS; MDTP; Math Mammoth |
| Integers & Negative Numbers | ops, absolute value, multi-step | NJSLS-6 NS; Math Mammoth |
| Order of Operations & Expressions | PEMDAS, evaluate, combine like terms | NJSLS-6 EE |
| Ratios, Rates & Proportions | proportions, unit rate, % change | NJSLS-6 RP |
| Exponents & Powers | powers, roots, exponent rules | NJSLS-6 EE; MDTP |
| Equations **& Inequalities** | 1- & 2-step equations, variables both sides, **inequalities** | NJSLS-6 EE |
| Coordinate Plane & Word Problems | quadrants, set-up-the-equation problems | NJSLS-6 NS/EE |
| **Number Theory (GCF, LCM, primes)** | GCF, LCM, primes, prime factorization, word problems | NJSLS-6 NS |
| **Patterns, Sequences & Tables** | arithmetic/geometric sequences, nth term, **function rules / input-output tables** | IAAT Part 3 (the defining readiness skill) |
| **Geometry & Measurement** | area, perimeter, circumference, volume, surface area, angles | NJSLS-6 Geometry; MDTP; Math Mammoth |
| **Data & Statistics** | mean, median, range, basic probability | NJSLS-6 Statistics |

## Difficulty calibration (3 levels)

- 🟢 **Medium** — regular public-school / grade-level (≈ Algebra-Class readiness, easy MDTP).
- 🟠 **Hard** — the real placement-test bar (≈ Math Mammoth Grade-7 "80% = ready", MDTP, the
  computational half of Orleans-Hanna/IAAT). **This is the most important tier.**
- 🔴 **Challenging** — beyond the test: hard algebra-readiness + Math-Kangaroo-style reasoning.
